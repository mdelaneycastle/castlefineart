Our story

Celebrating art for all

We believe everyone should be able to experience the joy of collecting art. Since 1995 we've grown into the UK's leading art retailer — with galleries nationwide and collectors around the world.

Since 1995

Thirty years of fantastic art

In 1995 we opened our first gallery in Stratford-upon-Avon with one goal: to make art accessible to all. Today we work with some of the biggest names in art — including Johnny Depp, Marvel and Bob Dylan — alongside homegrown talent whose careers we've helped launch.

Our motto says it simply: Your World, Our Art®.

Payment, delivery & care

Interest-free finance

Spread the cost of your artwork with 0% APR finance and no deposit, subject to status. Available online and in every gallery.

Free UK delivery

All artwork is made to order in Birmingham and delivered free across the UK. Please allow up to six weeks. International delivery is available on request.

Returns

If your artwork isn't quite right, most pieces can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Speak to your gallery or our customer team.

Framing & craftsmanship

Every piece is mounted, glazed or framed by hand, with bespoke framing chosen by our creative department to suit each work.
